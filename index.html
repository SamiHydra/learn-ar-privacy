This Privacy Policy explains how Learn AR handles information when you use the application, including camera access, local storage, and data processed by Google Play Services for AR.

Interpretation and Definitions

In this policy:

  • Application or Service means Learn AR, an educational augmented reality application with learning content for Grades 9 through 12.
  • Institute, we, us, or our means the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
  • Device means the phone or tablet on which you use Learn AR.
  • Personal data means information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
  • You means the person using Learn AR.
  • ARCore means Google Play Services for AR, the Google platform used for augmented reality on supported Android devices.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Personal Information and Accounts

Learn AR does not require an app account and does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number, or payment information to use its learning features. It does not include advertising or in-app purchases.

If you choose to contact us by email, you provide your email address and any information included in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, photographs, or videos.

Information Processed While Using the Application

Camera and AR tracking. Learn AR requests camera permission to recognize supported textbook images and learning markers and display educational models in the camera view. Camera images and tracking information are processed on your device. Learn AR does not include a feature that records, saves, or uploads camera photos or videos to the Institute. The current learning features do not require microphone recording or precise-location access.

Camera permission can be revoked in the device's app settings. Augmented reality features cannot work without it.

Local settings and performance display.

Learn AR stores your selected grade, theme, performance-display preference, and the latest image-detection timing locally on your device. It may also store a temporary copy of the bundled menu page. These values support the app's interface and performance display and are not sent to a developer-operated server by the app.

You can remove locally stored app preferences and cached files using Android's clear-storage controls. Device backup or restore features may preserve or restore app data according to your device settings and the backup provider's policies.

Usage Data and Third-Party Services

The current Learn AR configuration has Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity cloud crash reporting disabled. Learn AR does not operate its own account or analytics server. However, ARCore processes information separately as described below.

Google Play Services for AR

On supported Android devices, Learn AR uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google. Google documents that ARCore collects account identifiers when a user is signed into Google, or device identifiers otherwise, as well as performance diagnostics and API-usage information. Google uses this information to measure engagement, operate and diagnose ARCore, and improve augmented reality experiences. Google stores this information and encrypts ARCore's collected data in transit using HTTPS on supported Google Play devices.

Learn AR does not enable ARCore Cloud Anchors or use the Geospatial API in its current implementation.

Google's collection, retention, and deletion practices are governed by its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Play Services for AR handles your data
  • ARCore's data collection disclosure

Use of Information

Learn AR uses camera and tracking information to recognize markers and position educational models. It uses local preferences to restore your selected grade and display settings, and detection timing to show performance information. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to handle your inquiry. Google's uses of ARCore data are described above and in its linked policies.

Retention of Your Personal Data

Local preferences remain until cleared through device storage controls. Temporary menu files may be removed when the device clears the app cache. Backup copies, if any, are managed through your device's backup provider.

Learn AR does not maintain an Institute-operated repository of camera images or user profiles. Support correspondence is handled through the email service used for your inquiry. Contact us below with questions about retention of correspondence.

Google determines retention of the ARCore data it processes under its own policies; the Institute does not control those retention periods.

Transfer and Disclosure of Your Personal Data

Learn AR does not sell personal information or transmit camera imagery or locally stored preferences to an Institute-operated server. Google's processing of ARCore data and any international transfers are governed by its policies. If you contact support, the email services involved process your message according to their practices.

The Institute may need to disclose support correspondence when required by applicable law or a valid request from an authorized public authority.

Deleting Information and Your Choices

You can clear locally stored preferences through Android's app-storage settings, remove temporary files through its cache controls, and manage camera permission in your device settings. Device backups are managed through the backup provider.

There is no Learn AR user account to delete. To ask about information you voluntarily sent to support, including requests for access, correction, or deletion, email us below. Applicable legal retention requirements may affect deletion. Google's privacy controls apply to information Google processes independently.

Security of Your Personal Data

Learn AR relies on device and operating-system protections for local app storage. ARCore uses the transport protection described above. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Learn AR provides educational content for Grades 9 through 12. It does not ask students to create profiles or enter personal contact details and does not include messaging between users. Google Play Services for AR may still process the data described in this policy. Parents, guardians, and educators may contact us with questions about student privacy or information provided to support.

Links to Other Websites

Learn AR can open privacy-policy links in an external browser. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies when visiting them. This policy describes Learn AR and does not govern those external websites.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy as the application's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on the policy webpage and the last-updated date will be revised. Please review the policy periodically.
